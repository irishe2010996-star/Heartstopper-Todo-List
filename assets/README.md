# Leaf artwork

The four-leaf reference used in the working desktop is adapted from **Alice Oseman's original Heartstopper comics**, with colors changed for this widget. Source identification was supplied by Iris. An exact original panel, page number, or publication URL has not been provided.

- Original work and illustrator: **Alice Oseman — Heartstopper**.
- Official work page: <https://aliceoseman.com/heartstopper/>.
- The widget applies grayscale/tint recoloring and positions the leaves in the header.
- The artwork is separate from this repository's MIT-licensed code. Attribution does not grant an artwork redistribution license.

The image is not bundled in this public source import. To retain the exact appearance on your existing computer, keep your existing `soft-rainbow-leaves.png` next to `todo-render.ps1`. On another computer, supply an appropriately licensed four-leaf image in that location before setup. The current crop coordinates are authored for the original four-leaf strip; another image may require adapting the crop definitions in `todo-render.ps1`.

Personal wallpaper, television stills, and screenshots of private tasks are not included.
